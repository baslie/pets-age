### English (без ссылок — добавить в комментарий позже)

**Title:** TIL the "multiply by 7" rule is wrong — a 9-month-old puppy can already have babies, so how is that only 5 human years?

**Body:**
UC San Diego researchers studied 105 Labrador retrievers and found that dogs age way faster when young, then slow down.

Think about it: a 9-month-old puppy can already reproduce. That's clearly not a "5-year-old" in human terms.

Their findings based on DNA methylation (epigenetic clock):
- 1-year-old dog → ~30 human years
- 4-year-old dog → ~52 human years
- 7+ years → aging slows down significantly

Lead researcher Trey Ideker said about his own 6-year-old dog: "She still runs with me, but I'm now realizing that she's not as young as I thought she was."

How old are your dogs? I'm curious how this changes how you see them!
